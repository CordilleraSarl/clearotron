*A rubric to be reasoned with — not paraphrased away and not reduced to a lookup.* This framework RATES Demo Brand Owner matters (doc 50). It is **matrix-shaped**: reason the two inputs below in order — the Legal Risk Level, then the Dispute Type — and read the band off the matrix. **State the rating as the band word** (Very High / High / Medium / Manageable / Low); the numerals beside the bands are internal indices, never the output. ## Legal Risk Levels (probability adverse party prevails)

| Level | Probability | Description |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| **A** | 0–24% | Weak claim, negligible risk, strong defences available |
| **B** | 25–49% | Some credible claims but more likely to prevail; manageable |
| **C** | 51–60% | Better than even chance of adverse outcome; material risk, may need settlement |
| **D** | 61–74% | Likely adverse outcome; some credible defences but unsuccessful result expected |
| **E** | 75%+ | Strong claim against us; no or only weak defences |

> **The letter follows the words (consistency rule — apply to every rated finding).** State the likelihood of confusion in plain words *first* — "more likely than not they'd win", "better-than-even is not reached", "confusion is unlikely", "distinguishable as wholes" — **then pick the letter those words require**: better-than-even reached → **C–E**; not reached → **A or B** (B = 25–49%, "credible claim but we have the better of it"). The prose read and the A–E letter are **one judgment and may not disagree.**

## Dispute Type

| Type | What it means here |
|------|--------------------|
| **Registry** | An opposition or refusal before an office. Cost is bounded, the outcome is a registration, and a loss rarely stops trading. |
| **Commercial** | A demand or dispute between traders — coexistence, undertakings, a rebrand of one channel. |
| **Enforcement** | An injunction, a takedown that removes a listing, or proceedings that stop a launch. |

**Software and training sit differently.** A conflict in class 9 reaches the product itself and the app-store listing; a conflict in class 41 reaches the training arm alone, which can be renamed without touching the software. Weigh which one the goods actually collide in before choosing the Dispute Type — a class-41-only collision is rarely Enforcement.

## Bands

| Band | What it means for a launch decision |
|------|--------------------------------------|
| **Very High** | Do not launch on this mark. A conflict that would be enforced, in the goods that matter. |
| **High** | Launch only with a plan: clearance of the conflicting right, a narrowing, or a parallel name. |
| **Medium** | Material risk. Proceed with the conflict named to the business and a monitoring decision taken. |
| **Manageable** | Routinely accepted. Note it, and say what would change the answer. |
| **Low** | No practical obstacle on the evidence read. |

## The matrix

| Level ↓ / Type → | Registry | Commercial | Enforcement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Low | Low | Manageable |
| **B** | Low | Manageable | Medium |
| **C** | Manageable | Medium | High |
| **D** | Medium | High | Very High |
| **E** | High | Very High | Very High |

**Ceilings are honoured as written.** A Registry dispute does not reach Very High under this framework however strong the claim: the register can refuse an application without stopping the business, and a band that cannot distinguish those two outcomes is not telling the reader anything.

## What this customer wants said

- **The register first, then the common law.** Say what the register shows, then what the open-web picture adds — or, explicitly, that it settles nothing. A silent common-law half reads as a clean one.
- **An unread source is a fact about the search.** If a register, a language lane or a case-law source was not reached, that belongs in the body beside the finding it affects, not in a closing caveat.
- **Crowded fields, briefly.** Where a field is crowded, say so once with its consequence for distinctiveness and move on. A list of near-identical third-party marks is evidence, not analysis.
