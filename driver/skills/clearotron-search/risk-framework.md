# Risk framework — House default

- **Applies to:** any matter whose customer profile has no framework of its own (the firm-wide fallback).
- **Source:** house-authored. The firm-wide fallback rubric, completed by the reviewing lawyer 2026-08-31.
- **Bands, highest to lowest:** Very High · High · **Moderate** · Manageable. There is no "Low"; Manageable is the lowest band.
- **Entity in prose:** "the company" (generic).
- **Materiality threshold:** the client's defined materiality threshold; **default $1 million** where the client has not defined one.

*A rubric to be reasoned with — not paraphrased away and not reduced to a lookup.* This framework RATES the matter (doc 50): reason each conflict through the band definitions below — Legal position first, then Practical position, then Potential consequences — and the band follows the words. A conflict the company clearly wins with no material risk is **not a rated conflict** (there is no "Low" band; see `synthesis-rules.md`). The engine applies this framework on an advisory basis; the senior lawyer makes the final determination. The firm-wide reasoning discipline — the consumer-confusion gate, the dominant-element rule, crowded-field handling, G&S discipline — lives in [`synthesis-rules.md`](synthesis-rules.md) and applies under every framework.

## VERY HIGH RISK

- **Legal position.** Prior rights owner is **very likely to win**. No credible defences or risk-mitigation strategies available.
- **Practical position.** Obvious commercial overlap · anticipated actual confusion · famous prior mark · major competitor · known trademark aggressor · settlement outcome extremely unlikely.
- **Potential consequences.** Name change likely required, possibly on an urgent basis · monetary exposure **above the client's defined materiality threshold** (default $1 million) · PR consequences highly likely.

## HIGH RISK

- **Legal position.** Prior rights owner is **likely to win**. Some credible defences or risk-mitigation strategies available, but success is not expected.
- **Practical position.** Commercial adjacency · co-existence through settlement possible but may involve payment of money, use restrictions or other 'gives'.
- **Potential consequences.** Name change may be required, possibly on an urgent basis · monetary exposure **at or near the client's defined materiality threshold** (default $1 million) · PR consequences likely.

## MODERATE RISK

- **Legal position.** Prior rights owner is **likely to win**. Reasonable defences or risk-mitigation strategies available.
- **Practical position.** No obvious commercial overlap · risk-mitigation strategies are available — e.g. the prior mark is weak or vulnerable to attack for non-use · co-existence through settlement feasible but may involve payment of money, use restrictions or other 'gives'.
- **Potential consequences.** Name change may be required, possibly on an urgent basis · monetary exposure **below the client's defined materiality threshold** (default $1 million) · PR consequences possible · risk-mitigation budget likely required.

## MANAGEABLE RISK *(lowest band)*

- **Legal position.** Prior rights owner is **likely to lose**. Good defences or risk-mitigation strategies available.
- **Practical position.** No obvious commercial overlap · risk-mitigation strategies are available — e.g. the prior mark is weak or vulnerable to attack for non-use · possible nuisance claim or challenge by a known aggressor or industry competitor.
- **Potential consequences.** Name change is unlikely · registry conflict possible · monetary exposure **well below the client's defined materiality threshold** (default $1 million) · budget may be required for nuisance claims or prosecution costs · risk-mitigation steps rarely necessary.
