# Petcary — customer context (baseline)

**Who.** Petcary is a worldwide animal-health brand spanning veterinary pharmaceuticals and supplements
(Nice class 5), diagnostics and medical devices (10), AI-driven animal-health services (42), and veterinary
services (44). Clearance scope is worldwide.

**What matters.** Because the brand crosses regulated animal drugs, software/AI services, and professional
veterinary services at once, the most informative conflicts tend to sit on three boundaries — human-vs-animal
health, pharma-vs-supplement, and device-vs-software. A look-alike already sitting on an official animal-drug
register (FDA Animal Drugs@FDA / EMA / UK VMD) is a stronger real-world signal than a bare marketplace
listing, and a register hit in one jurisdiction is a prompt to look at the others.

**Channel facts (for coverage honesty, not a rule).** Class-5 use-evidence lives on the animal-drug registers
and the pet-pharmacy storefronts the grid sweeps. Classes 42 and 44 are services with no storefront — they
ride the implicit general-web search, so the absence of a marketplace cell for them is expected and is not a
coverage gap. Class-10 devices have no dedicated worldwide veterinary-device marketplace; they surface via the
general web plus incidental retail listings.

**Known coverage limits (carry as concerns / questions).** The register sweep covers US, EU, UK and Australia;
there is no clean, English-resolvable CN/Asia veterinary-drug register host suitable for a site search, so
CN/Asia register coverage is not represented in the grid and would need separate handling. Health Canada's
veterinary register sits under the broad canada.ca host and is a poor site-search target, so Canadian register
coverage is thin. These are gaps to disclose, not findings.

**Open questions for the analysis to carry.** Is a surfaced human-pharma or human-supplement mark a genuine
cross-class concern for the animal-health goods, or is it off-field? Does a near mark's animal-drug-register
status — listed vs not — change how heavily it should weigh against Petcary's goods?
