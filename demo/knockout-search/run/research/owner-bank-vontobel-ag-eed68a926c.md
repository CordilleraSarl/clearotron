## Findings

1. **What the bank does.** Bank Vontobel AG’s 2025 annual report identifies it as a Swiss-law public limited company and says it provides services from Zurich and branches in other Swiss cities. Its reported income includes commissions from securities trading and investment activities, lending and other services, trading results, and interest income. In 2025, for example, it reported CHF 515.5 million in commission income from securities trading and investment activities and CHF 386.3 million in results from trading activities and the fair-value option. [1]

2. **Products and activities documented in the report.** The report identifies Lombard lending and real-estate-secured mortgage loans to private clients; it says the bank does not in principle engage in commercial lending. It also states that the bank trades standardized and over-the-counter derivatives on its own and on clients’ accounts, principally involving interest rates, currencies, and equities or indices. The report specifically identifies structured products issued by the bank, with underlying exposures including equities, currencies, interest rates, precious metals, and cryptocurrencies. [1]

3. **How that fits Vontobel’s wider offering.** Vontobel describes itself as an investment firm serving private and institutional clients, offering active investment products and solutions across asset classes, along with structured solutions. Its private-client description includes discretionary and advisory wealth-management services. Those are descriptions of the Vontobel business more broadly; they should not all automatically be attributed to Bank Vontobel AG as a separate legal entity. [2][3]

4. **What can be said about VENQORI.** The supplied information says Bank Vontobel AG holds a registration in classes 9, 16, and 36, but I could not locate or verify a specific public record spelling out the VENQORI goods and services or showing that the bank currently markets a VENQORI-branded offering. Swissreg is the Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property’s official database for trade marks, but the available search results here did not provide a VENQORI record. [4] Therefore, I cannot responsibly connect the mark to a particular app, publication, financial product, or service.
