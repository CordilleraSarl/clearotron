## Summary

Public evidence indicates that a Rosario-based business called **Legado IT / Legado IT SRL** provides software and technology services, including custom software development, GeneXus consulting and development, and K2BTools licensing and related services. [1][2] I could not verify from the sources found that this public-facing business is the same legal entity as **“LEGADO IT S.R.L.-FERREYRA ESTEFANIA-CABRAL JUAN H.”**, or that it currently sells anything under the trademark **VENQORI**. [1][2]

## Findings

1. **Business activity described on Legado IT’s own website.** Legado IT describes itself as a technology-solutions company and says it develops custom software. It lists a “Security Manager” access-control and management system, and describes services including GeneXus project analysis, design, development, implementation, consulting, and management of GeneXus knowledge bases. It also describes K2BTools and GeneXus-related tools and solutions. [1]

2. **Licensing and services.** Legado IT’s LinkedIn company page says it is an official K2BTools agent, commercializes licenses, and provides related services; it lists GeneXus, K2BTools, ERP, and health as specialties. It identifies Rosario, Santa Fe, Argentina, and links to legadoit.com.ar. [2]

3. **Limits on connecting these findings to the named registrant or VENQORI.** The website and LinkedIn material identify the business as Legado IT (and the website refers to “Legado IT SRL”), but the material retrieved does not name Ferreyra Estefania or Cabral Juan H. or mention VENQORI. [1][2] Therefore, these sources support a description of the Legado IT business, but do **not** establish that the named trademark registrant is that same business or that VENQORI is one of its current products or services. [1][2]

## Gaps

- I could not verify the legal-entity match between “LEGADO IT S.R.L.-FERREYRA ESTEFANIA-CABRAL JUAN H.” and the Legado IT website or LinkedIn page. [1][2]
- I could not find evidence of what VENQORI specifically denotes or whether it is actively marketed or traded today. The trademark registration in classes 9 and 42, by itself, does not establish actual current sales. [1][2]
- The official website describes offerings but does not provide a clear publication or update date in the retrieved content, so the precise currentness of each listed offering cannot be independently confirmed. [1]
