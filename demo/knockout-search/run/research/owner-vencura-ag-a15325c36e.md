## Summary

The strongest verifiable description of **vencura AG** is that it is a Basel company whose registered purpose is to advise on insurance and arrange insurance products in Switzerland. The available sources do not establish which insurance products it currently sells, whether it is actively making sales, or any separate business behind the **VENQORI** mark. The trademark classes alone do not show what the company actually trades in.

## Findings

### What the company is registered to do

1. The company-register information reproduced by Moneyhouse identifies vencura AG as active, based in Basel, and registered in 2022. Its stated purpose is, in translation, **insurance-sector consulting and the mediation/arrangement of insurance products in Switzerland**. The same source also describes broader powers—such as setting up or acquiring companies and providing financing—but those are permissions in the stated corporate purpose, not evidence that it actually carries on those activities. [1]

2. A separate Basel company-register listing gives the same insurance-advice and insurance-product-mediation purpose and identifies the company by UID **CHE-275.154.556**. [2]

### What it actually sells or trades in

3. These sources establish the company’s **declared business area**, but do not identify particular insurance policies or other products sold by vencura AG, name customers, or document transactions. Moneyhouse’s company page likewise gives the purpose, but does not provide a concrete product catalogue or evidence of current sales. [1]

4. Sympany’s website describes insurance products and advice offered by **Sympany**, including health, accident, property and liability insurance. The material found does not say that vencura AG itself offers those products, so it cannot establish that they are vencura AG’s current offerings. [3][4]

### What the VENQORI registration establishes

5. The company page’s trademark list names **“vencura”**, not “VENQORI”; it does not give VENQORI’s owner or goods-and-services wording. I could not verify from the material retrieved that the VENQORI registration belongs to vencura AG. [1]

6. The Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property explains that a trademark application specifies the goods and/or services for which protection is sought. That makes a registration relevant to the scope of claimed trademark protection, but it does not by itself establish that the registrant currently sells or provides those goods or services. [5]

## Gaps

- I could not verify the **VENQORI** registration’s owner, exact goods-and-services description, or current status from the sources retrieved. The user-provided classes were therefore not independently confirmed.
- I found no direct company source, product list, or transaction evidence establishing what specific insurance products vencura AG currently sells or whether it is presently operating a sales business.
- **Bottom line:** insurance advice and arranging insurance products in Switzerland are established as the company’s registered purpose. Beyond that, what vencura AG actually sells today cannot be established from the available sources.
