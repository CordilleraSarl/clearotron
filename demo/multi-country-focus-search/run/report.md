## Align Networks Inc. — ENQOR, KR
- ord: 2
- net: Align Networks' pending enqor applications are more likely than not to fail as an objection to VENQORI in Korea, though they cover the same downloadable app software (among broad goods).
- group: on-field
- source: Register
### Full detail

- **Portfolio.** Align Networks Inc., a Korean company, holds three pending applications for enqor filed as word-and-design marks in classes 9, 41 and 42, all filed on the same date in April 2026.
- **Risk assessment.** enqor and VENQORI share a rare letter sequence but read as different wholes: their Korean transliterations differ at the opening syllable, a difference Korean examination practice weighs heavily, and VENQORI carries a third syllable enqor does not have. - **Enforcement.** The three applications remain pending rather than registered and were filed as word-and-design, so a design element may carry part of the distinctiveness and the word-only conflict is narrower than the class overlap suggests. What they threaten is the examiner raising the enqor applications during examination of a Korean filing, and a possible opposition in classes 9, 41 and 42, neither dependent on which side reaches Korean consumers first.
