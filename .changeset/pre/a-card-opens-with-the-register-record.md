New: On a clearance report, each conflict now opens its cited registrations with the register's own record, before the analysis. A closed "Register record" drop-down lists, for each registration, the mark and its type, the goods and services, the owner and the owner's country, the country, the status with its dates, and the register number. Goods show in English: the register's own where it has it, otherwise the provider's machine translation, marked as one. A card lists up to eight registrations and points to the audit workbook for the rest. Drop-down titles now read clearly in the dark theme.
