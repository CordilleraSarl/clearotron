Catch a report claiming it searched a territory the run never searched

The masthead's coverage line is computed by the driver; the narrative around it is
written by the model. Nothing compared the two, so a report could state one scope in
its header and a wider one in its prose — and on one delivered run it did, until a
person noticed by eye. Both now answer to the same record of what was searched, and a
disagreement is caught and repaired before the client is handed the report.
